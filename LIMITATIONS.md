# Limitations (v5.1)

1. **G-Sec series provenance.** The NSE Total Return Index endpoint could not be reached from the build environment (HTTP 403), so the series used is the Investing.com export "Nifty GS Composite" supplied by the author (2016-04-05 to 2024-12-31, 2,158 observations after excluding one Saturday row). It is not confirmed to be the NSE Total Return Index (its 7.3% a year growth is consistent with a coupon-accruing index but is not proof) and contains a few isolated one-day dips that reverse (for example -3.3% on 2022-09-12) that may be bad prints; they are kept as supplied. It ends 2024-12-31, so the historical scenario and H1/H3 stop there and the live optimiser estimates the debt and gold covariances with the funds on only 429 overlapping days. `tools/fetch_gsec_nse.py` can obtain the official series on a computer with access; it is tested on mock responses only.
2. **The 10% cap and the 50% equity floor conflict in some historical years.** In 2021, 2022 and 2023 even 50% equity cannot meet 10% ex-ante (10.7% to 11.6%) because the trailing window includes the 2020 crash; those years are flagged cap breaches and the realised volatility of the dynamic portfolio is 10.3%. The live optimiser instead lowers equity (never below 50%) or reports infeasibility.
3. **Debt is often 0% and gold large.** With no floors, the optimiser prefers gold (historical mean 11.6% a year since 2010) to debt (7.2%) whenever the cap leaves room; the current recommendation is 72.5% equity, 0% debt, 27.5% gold. Gold's historical mean is not a forecast and the 429-day overlap for its correlations is short. Stress these inputs before relying on the split.
4. **Optimiser return and Sharpe are historical, not forecasts.** Fund returns (about 19% a year even after shrinkage toward an assumed 12% prior) come from a 3.75 year bull market; the portfolio return (16.9%) and Sharpe (1.04) displayed are therefore optimistic. The weights depend only on relative returns and the covariance structure.
5. **Short fund history.** The 12 funds share only 921 daily returns (2023-02-23 to 2026-11-25); average pairwise correlation is about 0.90. Fund weights are weakly identified.
6. **Hypothesis evidence is limited.** H1 is directional only (interval includes zero; sign changes across sensitivities). H2 holds in-sample by about 0.3 pp but not in the hold-out. H3 holds in every variant but rests on one seven-year window with one crash.
7. **Monte Carlo**: normal monthly returns (no fat tails or volatility clustering), annual rebalancing, equity mean from a bull-market window. It is a simulation, not performance or a forecast.
8. **Data issues disclosed in `data/DATA_QUALITY_REPORT.md`**: Nifty TRI ends 2024-12-31; the gold series is USD spot x INR/USD without duties; P/B series break in 2023-09.
9. No transaction costs, taxes or exit loads.
10. The bundled fund NAV data are as supplied by the project author; no independent verification against AMFI was possible here.
