## The debt series (Investing.com export, ingested)
See `docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md` for the validation of the supplied file (2,159 rows, one Saturday row excluded, 2,158 kept, 2016-04-05 to 2024-12-31). The text below records the earlier state and the NSE retrieval attempt.

The NSE "about" sheet holds no daily levels and was not used as data. No debt series was created, proxied or substituted.
Retrieval attempt (27 Nov 2026): a request to the NSE Total Return Index endpoint (`niftyindices.com/Backpage.aspx/getTotalReturnIndexString`) was blocked in the build environment (HTTP 403, host not permitted), so no response, row count or checksum exists. A public Mendeley dataset of G-Sec index log returns ends in March 2018 and is not the Total Return Index level series, so it was not used. `tools/fetch_gsec_nse.py` downloads and validates the series on a computer with access (untested against the live endpoint).
Consequence: the optimiser, Monte Carlo and scenario use the supplied G-Sec series (historical). The series ends on 2024-12-31, so the scenario ends there and the funds/debt/gold covariance overlap is 2023-02-23 to 2024-12-31 (429 observations).

## Dataset checks

### Nifty 50 TRI (2014-01-01 to 2024-12-31, 2,721 observations)
* Duplicate dates 0, nulls 0, non-positive values 0. Observations per year 244 to 252.
* Largest calendar gap 6 days; one gap above 5 days (2014-10-07).
* Ten rows fall on a weekend (special sessions, for example 2020-02-01 Budget Saturday); they are real NSE sessions and are kept.
* Largest daily moves are all in March to April 2020 (-12.9% on 2020-03-23). No change was made.
* One zero-return day, 2014-05-14 (identical level to the prior day). It may be a stale print. It is kept and flagged, not corrected.
* The series ends 2024-12-31, so the historical evaluation window cannot extend beyond that date.

### Nifty valuation (monthly)
* Convention: a reading for month M is dated at the end of month M. The last row is dated by the file's as-of date (2026-11-26).
  In the scenario a decision on 31 Dec of Y-1 uses only the last reading dated on or before that day.
* Full-sample mean and standard deviation: P/E 20.87 and 4.55; P/B 3.59 and 0.74. The synopsis constants (P/E 22 and 5, P/B 3.2 and 0.6) differ from these.
  The model therefore uses point-in-time expanding statistics as the primary choice and the synopsis constants as a reported sensitivity.
* Discontinuity: P/B falls from 4.40 (2023-08) to 3.45 (2023-09) while P/E barely moves (22.0 to 22.2). This looks like a change in the provider's book-value basis. It is kept as supplied and flagged because it affects any P/B-based signal.

### Gold (2010-05-07 to 2026-11-19, 3,536 observations)
* Duplicates 0, nulls 0. 2010 to 2013 are sparse (30, 162, 102, 132 observations per year; gaps up to 65 days). From 2014 the series is daily (about 240 per year).
* This is international spot converted to rupees. Import duty, GST and making charges are excluded, so it is not the price an Indian investor pays. This is a stated limitation.
* Daily moves above 5% occur in 2011 to 2013, 2020 and in 2026-01 to 2026-03. They were kept and are listed in the JSON report.

### Fund NAVs (12 schemes)
* Each scheme: duplicates 0, non-positive NAV 0, nulls 0, one scheme-name variant. Identity is validated against the scheme codes and names in `universe.py` (wrong plan, option or category is rejected).
* Coverage against business days is about 94%; the largest gap is 6 days (the same 2014-10-07 gap appears in the TRI file; its cause was not determined and nothing was filled).
* Inception dates differ. ITI starts 2023-02-22, WhiteOak 2022-08-03, Invesco 2022-02-16; the others have at least five years.

## How inception differences are handled
* Optimisation, covariance and the common-period table use the common window of all 12 funds: 2023-02-23 to 2026-11-25 (921 daily returns, about 3.75 years).
* Each fund's own-window metrics (up to the 1,260 most recent observations, about five years) are also reported. They are not comparable across funds because the windows differ.
* Missing days are never filled and never turned into zero returns. A return is formed only between two consecutive observed NAV dates, and returns across gaps longer than 7 calendar days or above 15% in absolute size are excluded and counted.

## Known limitations
1. The common fund window is short (about 3.75 years) and is a strong bull period: most common-window CAGRs are 15% to 24%, which is not a long-run expectation.
2. The 12-fund average pairwise correlation is about 0.90, so diversification inside the sleeve is limited and weights are poorly identified.
3. The TRI file ends 2024-12-31 and the fund data end 2026-11-25. Beta and alpha versus the TRI use only the overlap (to 2024-12-31).
4. Debt is not available (see above).
